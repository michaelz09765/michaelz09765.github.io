Privacy policy

Solutions Books uploader · last updated 9 October 2026

Who runs it

Michael Zefi (michaelzefi@gmail.com) runs Solutions Books uploader for his own use. Nobody else signs in to it.

What it does

The uploader copies the study guides Michael writes from his computer to a folder in his own Google Drive, and shares that folder so anyone with the link can view it.

What Google data it uses

It asks Google for one permission, drive.file: access to the files and folders the uploader itself creates. It cannot see any other file in the Drive. It collects no personal data, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics. The Google sign-in token stays on Michael's computer and goes nowhere else.

Students who open the shared folder use Google Drive directly. The uploader never learns who opened it.

The uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and deletion

The uploader shares no data with anyone. Michael can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
